Frequently Asked Questions

What if my schedule changes and I can't follow my routine?

Your routine should adapt when life changes. We'll create realistic alternatives for busy days, travel and unexpected situations, so missing one workout or meal prep session doesn't mean starting over.

I don't have enough time to cook. How will I manage my diet?

You don't need to cook elaborate meals or spend hours meal prepping. We'll build practical meals around your schedule, available foods and cooking preferences, using simple combinations and convenient options where needed.

My previous nutritionist made me feel hungry. Will your diet plan be the same?

You shouldn't have to rely on excessive drinks or eat too little just to manage your weight. We'll focus on satisfying meals, appropriate portions, adequate protein and fibre, and foods you enjoy so your nutrition supports your goals without leaving you constantly hungry.

Do I need to count every gram of protein or follow a strict diet?

No. Your nutrition should meet your individual needs without making eating a full-time job. We'll focus on balanced meals, appropriate protein intake and realistic portions rather than rigid targets that don't suit your lifestyle.

What if I've tried multiple plans but always lose consistency?

Instead of labelling you undisciplined, we'll identify what's getting in the way—whether it's time, restrictive eating, exhaustion or unrealistic expectations and adapt the plan to address the actual problem.

Will I need to feel sore or exhausted to get results?

No. Soreness isn't a measure of workout quality. Your training will progress according to your ability, technique and recovery, helping you get stronger without unnecessarily compromising your everyday life.

Will I become dependent on you to maintain my results?

No. My goal is to teach you how to make decisions independently. You'll learn how to adjust your meals, workouts and habits as your needs change, so you can continue looking after yourself even after coaching ends.
