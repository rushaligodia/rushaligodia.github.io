Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to follow a strict protein requirement like 2g per kg?

No. We focus on balanced meal plans you enjoy, prioritizing protein-rich breakfasts and realistic portion control without rigid, unachievable rules.

What if I have busy workdays and zero time for long gym sessions?

My workouts are designed specifically for time crunches. We focus on quality movement, proper posture, breathing, and core engagement so you get results without spending hours exercising.

Will I be able to keep up this routine after coaching ends?

Yes! My entire coaching goal is to teach you how to exercise independently and eat healthily in my absence. You also get access to my book on sustainability.
