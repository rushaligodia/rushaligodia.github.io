Hi, I'm Rushali

At 15, I lost my father to an intestine attack. Experiencing that level of grief made me realize how delicate health is, and it sparked my lifelong mission: preventing families from suffering preventable diseases and lifestyle breakdowns.

When I began my own journey, I struggled with body confidence and encountered a fitness world full of harshness—coaches who blamed clients, demanded 2g of protein per kg, forced joint-destroying workouts, and normalized severe period pain.

My Goals

My goal as a coach is to help you build functional strength, move with freedom, and gain an unshakable confidence that carries into every part of your life. I care far more about how well you feel and move for decades to come than I do about quick fixes or extreme, rigid rules. Fitness should seamlessly support your life—giving you the energy to keep up with your kids, feel strong in everyday moments, and live actively and independently—rather than take it over.

By combining deep professional expertise across exercise, nutrition, and lifestyle coaching, we focus on creating a realistic, empowering foundation tailored to your unique routine. Whether you are completely new to fitness, navigating motherhood, or looking for a sustainable, science-backed approach to your health, I am dedicated to equipping you with the strength and habits needed to thrive for a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to follow a strict protein requirement like 2g per kg?

No. We focus on balanced meal plans you enjoy, prioritizing protein-rich breakfasts and realistic portion control without rigid, unachievable rules.

What if I have busy workdays and zero time for long gym sessions?

My workouts are designed specifically for time crunches. We focus on quality movement, proper posture, breathing, and core engagement so you get results without spending hours exercising.

Will I be able to keep up this routine after coaching ends?

Yes! My entire coaching goal is to teach you how to exercise independently and eat healthily in my absence. You also get access to my book on sustainability.
